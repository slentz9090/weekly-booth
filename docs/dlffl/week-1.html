On The Record

BOOMER: A call on every team, in the order I would rather own these rosters right now. Twelve on the board, none of them graded yet. Next week I read every one of them back, and when I am wrong I say so before I say anything else.

  1. DA The Badenov's. DA finishes top three in scoring again in Week 2.

    Josh Allen at 38.66, Christian Watson at 33.7, Kyle Monangai at 23.4, and 3.70 wasted. That is not one hot player carrying a roster, that is three of them and a manager who did not get in the way. It does not take 156 to finish top three.

  2. Momma's Boys. Mama Beth beats DA on Sunday.

    She is 17 and 10 against him across 27 meetings, and 12 and 10 against Casey Couture. Only two people in this building own more rings than she does, and one of them is the man she just watched lose by 43. She does not need a career week to beat DA. She needs a Sunday.

  3. Berlin Blitz. Ja'Marr Chase goes for 15 or more for Kent Frenger in Week 2.

    Two points from a receiver taken that early is not a thing that repeats. Frenger won by fifty without him. Sunday we find out whether that was a bye week or a warning.

  4. One Under akaThe Mulligan. Casey Couture is 2 and 0 by Sunday night.

    Five rings and the luckiest man in this league's history, six wins above expectation. He does not have to be good. He has to be present.

  5. Balls Deep. Spencer Lentz beats Adam Calvelage by more than thirty.

    Fifth in points and 0 and 1. Seven of the ten teams he did not play would have lost to his 114.02. He drew the one man who went for 156.

  6. BearDown. Ryan Reed starts Jaxson Dart in Week 2.

    Reed had not played a game in this league since 2021 and his 108.26 is already above his career median of 101.78. One list, two names. Now we find out whether he can read.

  7. Samba St. Gibbs Lion King. Scott Reisert does not reach 120 in Week 2 or Week 3.

    Seventy-two games into his career here he averages 101.62. He scored 104.94 and got a win. That is the plan and it is working, and I still do not believe in it.

  8. Cosby Copperheads. Doug Fields outscores Ryan Reed on Sunday.

    Fields has never lost to Ryan Reed. Three games, three wins. He also went 13 and 1 two years ago, the best record any champion has posted here. A 96.96 is not who this man is.

  9. Holy Rollers. Adam Hershberger beats Geoff Cavender by twenty or more.

    He made the best move in the league and the reward was the third-highest score in it sitting across the table. Now he gets the lowest winning score in the league instead. That is the whole schedule, right there.

  10. Hawk Tua Tagovailoa. Geoff Cavender does not clear 110 in Week 2.

    Undefeated at 96.40, below his own career median of 98.92, and nine of the ten teams he did not play would have beaten him. The W is the only evidence in his favor.

  11. The Scranton Strangler. Scott Howard scores 120 or more in Week 2.

    Yes, I am betting on him. Lamar Jackson gave him 28.96, he had 126.26 sitting in the house, and he turned in 80.26 of it. Nobody on this list has a shorter walk to a big number. Sunday we find out whether he takes it.

  12. An alBum Cover. Adam Calvelage clears 100 in Week 2.

    Last in the league, and he released 41.2 points off his own roster to get there. He is down to two quarterbacks and he has already found the floor. A hundred is not a big ask. It is the smallest one on this page.
