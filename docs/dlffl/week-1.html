Dennis Couture put up 156.86, the most in the league. Beth Couture put up 149.12. Kent Frenger was third at 147.76 and after that the whole league falls off a cliff — nobody else cleared 130. Week 2 puts the Coutures across the table from each other.

  5. Balls Deep. 114.02, fifth most in the league, and he drew the highest number in it. Four men beat that score. Only one of them was standing across from him.
