A Husband And Wife Put Up The Two Biggest Scores In This League. Next Sunday They Play Each Other.

DA went for 156.86, the most in the league. Mama Beth went for 149.12. Kent Frenger was third at 147.76 and then the whole league falls off a cliff — nobody else cleared 130.

The whole week in 35 seconds

On The Record

BOOMER: Four calls. I put them at the top so nobody can pretend I buried them. Every one gets graded in this spot next week, in public, and when I am wrong I say so before I say anything else.

1

Mama Beth beats DA on Sunday.

She is 17 and 10 against him across 27 meetings and she did not need a career week to do it. He just had one and it still only got him 7.74 more than her.

2

Scott Reisert does not clear 120 points in September.

Jahmyr Gibbs scored 34.1 of his 104.94. Take Gibbs out and there are 70.84 points of roster behind him. I do not believe in it.

3

Ryan Reed starts the wrong quarterback again before Halloween.

He owns Justin Herbert and Jaxson Dart. He has to pick one off a list of two every Sunday for six more weeks. Week 1 cost him 13.34 of the 21.18 he lost by.

4

Scott Howard leaves twenty or more on his bench again in Week 2.

Not because he is unlucky. Because he has a roster full of answers and a habit of asking the wrong one.

GIL: Recorded, all four.

Boomer's Power Rankings

BOOMER: Twelve teams. My order. One line each, and every line is something I have not already said.

  1. DA The Badenov's. The unluckiest owner in the recorded history of this league, six wins below what his scores deserved. He is not going to score 156 every week, and he does not have to.
  2. Momma's Boys. Three starters within three tenths of each other and nothing on the bench. That roster has no single point of failure, which is the rarest thing in this league.
  3. Berlin Blitz. The defending champion won by fifty with his first receiver scoring 2.2. Everybody else needs that to be who Chase is now.
  4. One Under akaThe Mulligan. Five rings and he is the luckiest man in league history, six wins above expectation. Casey Couture does not need to be good. He needs to be present.
  5. Balls Deep. Fifth in points and 0 and 1, because he drew the only score in the league he could not have beaten.
  6. BearDown. Two defenses' worth of good process and one quarterback slot he cannot stop losing.
  7. Samba St. Gibbs Lion King. 1 and 0 and six teams outscored him. If Gibbs has a quiet Sunday there is nothing behind it.
  8. Cosby Copperheads. Derrick Henry at 37.8 and a defense at negative four. Doug Fields is one waiver claim away from being a problem.
  9. Holy Rollers. Best move in the league, worst luck in the league. He gets Cavender next week and he should win by thirty.
  10. Hawk Tua Tagovailoa. Undefeated and he would have lost to nine of the eleven teams he did not play.
  11. The Scranton Strangler. The roster is not the problem. I have seen the bench.
  12. An alBum Cover. Last in scoring, and he released 41.2 points off his own roster to get there.

GIL: Next Sunday: DA at Mama Beth. Also Geoff Cavender at Adam Hershberger, Kent Frenger at Scott Reisert, Scott Howard at Casey Couture, Adam Calvelage at Spencer Lentz, and Ryan Reed at Doug Fields.
