The Freiermuth Rule

GIL: Named for Week 1, when Bob Dorsch sat Pat Freiermuth's 13.10 and lost by 0.46. Do not leave the answer on your own bench. The test is one swap: a player you sat, for the player you started in his place, worth more than you lost by. This week nobody failed it.

GIL: Aaron Jezioro came closest. Tucker Kraft for Jalen Coker is worth 13.50, and he lost by 14.18. He passed by 0.68.
