Aron Rogers Beat Aaron Jezioro By 75 Points. It Was The Ninth Straight.

Halftime in Friends of Herb. Aron Rogers set a perfect lineup, scored 152.12, beat Aaron Jezioro 152.12 to 76.82 for the ninth straight time, and finished second in the league for the week by 0.56. Michael Turner cut Cam Skattebo and signed Tre Tucker. Ryan Kelly signed Skattebo. They played each other, each man benched his new man for about twenty points, and Kelly won his first game of the year. The booth opens the archive on what the halfway mark has meant since 2011, and Herb is in it.
